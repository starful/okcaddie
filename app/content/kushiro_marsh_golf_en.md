## Course Overview

Kushiro Marsh Golf sits at the edge of one of Japan's best-known wetland areas, and that setting defines the round more than any single design flourish. Expect open, low-lying terrain with long sightlines across grassland and marsh rather than heavily bunkered target golf. Turf tends toward cool-season grasses suited to Hokkaido's shorter, milder summer, so fairways can play firmer in dry stretches and softer after rain.

Wind is the variable to plan around here. Because the course borders open wetland, gusts move across the layout with little to block them, and Kushiro's coastal fog can roll in during summer mornings, especially near the water-adjacent holes. Elevation change is generally modest, so the challenge leans on wind management and shot placement around the marsh line rather than dramatic uphill or downhill holes. This is a course that rewards patience and club selection over raw power.

## Access

The nearest airport is Kushiro Airport (KUH), which connects to Tokyo and other domestic hubs with regular flights; from there the course is a manageable drive rather than a long transfer. If you're coming from Kushiro's train station or downtown area, a car remains the most practical option given the course's position near the wetland outskirts.

Taxis can work for a one-off round, but the reality is that public transit doesn't reach directly to most Hokkaido golf courses, and taxi fares for a round trip plus wait time add up quickly. Renting a car for your Hokkaido leg, or arranging a hotel shuttle if one is offered, is the more realistic plan for golfers without their own vehicle.

## Dress Code & Tips

Standard Japanese golf course etiquette applies: collared shirts, tailored golf trousers or shorts, and soft spikes only — metal spikes are not accepted at most clubhouses. Bring a layer even in summer, since marsh-adjacent mornings can be cooler and damper than inland courses.

A few practical tips: book earlier in the week if you want a weekend slot during peak summer months, since local demand rises alongside visitor traffic; pack wind-resistant outerwear given how exposed parts of the layout are; and build extra buffer time into your schedule for the drive out, since this isn't a course you can reach on short notice from central Kushiro.
