## Course Overview

Expect an open, breathing layout that leans on the lake and surrounding Hokkaido terrain for character rather than manufactured drama. Turf in this part of Hokkaido tends to favor cool-season grasses, so fairways are usually at their best in mid-summer and can be firmer or slower depending on how early or late in the season you play. Elevation change is generally modest, but wind off the lake is the variable to respect — it can shift club selection more than the yardage card suggests, especially in the afternoon. Sightlines toward the water are a recurring feature rather than a one-hole gimmick, which is the main draw for casual and mid-handicap players who want scenery with their round. Don't expect a hole-by-hole highlight reel here; the appeal is the overall setting and the pace, not a signature stretch.

## Access

The nearest airport is Memanbetsu Airport, which serves Abashiri with a drive of roughly 20–30 minutes depending on traffic and exact course location. JR Abashiri Station is the relevant rail hub if you're routing by train from elsewhere in Hokkaido, with a taxi or transfer needed to reach the course itself. A rental car is the practical choice for this trip — public transit options thin out once you're outside central Abashiri, and having your own car gives you flexibility for the surrounding sightseeing stops. Taxis are workable for a single round trip but add up quickly if you're staying multiple days or combining golf with other stops around the lake.

## Dress Code & Tips

Standard golf attire applies: collared shirts, tailored shorts or trousers, and golf shoes with soft spikes only — check with the pro shop if you're unsure about a specific piece of clothing. Three practical tips: first, book your tee time a few days ahead in peak summer, since good weather windows fill fast. Second, pack a windbreaker even in summer — lake-adjacent wind can make mornings and evenings noticeably cooler than the daytime forecast suggests. Third, build in buffer time for the drive from Memanbetsu Airport or Abashiri Station, since local road conditions and rental car pickup can eat more time than expected on a tight schedule.
