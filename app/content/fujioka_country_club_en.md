## Course Overview

Fujioka's reputation comes from its tournament history rather than from dramatic water features or forced carries. Expect a layout that rewards accurate ball placement over the fairway, with greens that demand real putting discipline. The Gotemba setting means some elevation change and seasonal wind, particularly in the shoulder seasons when Fuji-area weather shifts quickly. Turf conditions on a historic private club like this tend to be tightly maintained, with firmer fairways in dry autumn stretches and softer, lusher turf after the early-summer rains. There's no need to invent hole-by-hole yardages here — the honest takeaway is that this is a classically designed course built for shot-making and course management, not a modern bomber's layout. ## Access

Fujioka Country Club is in Gotemba, Shizuoka, within the broader Hakone–Fuji Five Lakes travel zone. The nearest major air gateway is Tokyo, roughly 90 minutes to 2 hours by car from central Tokyo depending on traffic on the Tomei Expressway, with Gotemba being a well-known interchange point for that route. By train, the nearest useful hub is Gotemba Station on the JR Gotemba Line, though from there a taxi or a pre-arranged club shuttle is the realistic last leg since many private clubs in this area aren't set up for walk-in access from the station. Renting a car is the most flexible option for golfers combining this round with a Hakone or Fuji Five Lakes itinerary, but a taxi booked through your hotel is a reasonable alternative if you'd rather not navigate Japanese expressway signage. Build in extra buffer time if you're playing early morning, since the Tomei Expressway can see weekend congestion near Gotemba, especially during peak autumn foliage season.

## Dress Code & Etiquette Tips

Expect traditional Japanese private-club standards: collared shirts, tailored golf trousers or shorts with proper golf socks, and soft spikes only — metal spikes are not accepted. Denim and overly casual streetwear are typically turned away at check-in, so pack accordingly if this is one stop on a broader Japan trip. A few practical tips: first, confirm with your introducing member or booking contact whether a caddie is mandatory, since many historic Japanese clubs still require one, which affects both pace of play and total cost. Second, arrive well before your tee time — private clubs often expect guests to check in, change, and warm up on a specific schedule rather than showing up minutes before. Third, keep pace of play tight and be mindful of groups behind you; traditional clubs value steady rhythm over leisurely rounds, and your sponsoring member's reputation can be affected by your pace and etiquette.
