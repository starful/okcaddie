## Course Overview

Golf5 Country Sendai Izumi sits in the rolling, semi-suburban terrain north of central Sendai, with a layout built around comfortable sightlines rather than blind shots or severe doglegs. As a Golf5 Country property, the course is positioned for accessible, repeatable rounds — practice areas and a pro shop are typically on site, and the pace favors players who want to keep moving rather than grind over tricky recoveries.

Turf conditions follow the Tohoku climate: fairways and greens are at their best from late spring through autumn, with cooler-season grasses slowing down and going dormant-toned in winter. Wind is generally moderate rather than a dominant factor, and elevation changes are gentle — more rolling than mountainous — so the course plays fair for walkers and cart users alike. Expect a layout that rewards straight, manageable shots over high-risk aggression, which is part of why it suits beginners and mid-handicap groups so well.

## Green Fees & Booking (グリーン料金・予約)

Expect weekday green fees to run roughly in the ¥5,000–¥9,000 range and weekends/holidays higher, often ¥9,000–¥14,000, though this swings with season, time of day, and any seasonal campaigns. Packages at public courses like this typically bundle cart rental and sometimes a locker fee; caddie service, meals, and range balls are usually optional add-ons rather than automatic inclusions.

Most overseas and domestic visitors book through Rakuten GORA or a similar Japanese tee-time platform, and some hotels in Sendai can arrange a round through their concierge desk. Because fee boards and online rates both change with the season and local demand, always pull a live quote close to your travel dates rather than planning around a fixed number. ## Access from Sendai & the Airport

The course sits in Izumi Ward on the north side of Sendai, which puts it within a manageable drive of both the city center and Sendai Airport. From Sendai Station, plan on roughly 30–45 minutes by car depending on traffic; from Sendai Airport, figure closer to 45–60 minutes given the airport's position south of the city.

A rental car is the realistic way to reach this course — there's no direct train line to the clubhouse, and relying on taxis for the full airport-to-course run will add up quickly over a multi-day trip. If you're staying centrally in Sendai without a car, a taxi for the single trip is workable, but arrange the return trip in advance or confirm the course can call one for you, since rural taxi availability can be thin outside peak hours.

## Dress Code & Tips

Standard Japanese public-course etiquette applies: collared shirts, proper golf trousers or tailored shorts, and golf shoes with soft spikes (metal spikes are typically not allowed). Denim and plain gym wear are usually off-limits in the clubhouse and on the course.

- Book an early-morning slot if you can — it's cooler, less crowded, and gives you buffer time if pace is slower than expected
- Pack a light layer even in warmer months; Tohoku mornings can run cooler than the afternoon forecast suggests
- Confirm whether your booking includes a cart or assumes walking, since that changes both pace and what you need to carry
