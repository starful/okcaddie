Toyama Kokusai Golf sits in the hills outside Toyama City, offering a straightforward day of golf without the price tag of the resort-heavy circuits further south. It's the kind of course locals return to for a solid, well-paced round rather than a headline attraction.

## Who This Course Is For

- Golfers who want a genuine mountain-terrain test — elevation changes, tree-lined corridors, and a course that rewards shot placement over raw length.
- Travelers building a Hokuriku itinerary who want a reasonably priced round between Toyama City sightseeing and onsen time.
- Groups looking for a relaxed, less crowded weekday round outside peak Tokyo/Osaka golf traffic.
- Skip it if you're chasing a signature-designer layout or ocean-view holes — this is a workmanlike inland mountain course, not a showcase venue.
- Skip it if you need trolley-only, flat walking terrain; the elevation shifts are part of the character here.

## Course Overview

The layout follows the natural contours of the Toyama hillside, so expect uphill approaches, downhill run-outs, and greens that sit at varied elevations relative to the fairway. Turf tends toward firmer, well-drained conditions typical of mountain courses in this region — good roll in dry stretches, more mud-check carry in early spring or after rain. Wind is generally moderate and terrain-shaped rather than open and gusty, since tree lines and ridges break up crosswinds on most holes. Greens are moderately sized and slope with the hillside, so local knowledge of the fall lines helps more than raw putting speed. This isn't a course built around one or two showpiece holes — it's a steady, mid-length round where course management and short-game touch matter more than driver distance.

## Green Fees & Booking

Expect roughly ¥6,000–¥9,000 on weekdays and ¥9,000–¥14,000 on weekends and holidays, though these figures move with season, time of day, and cart-vs-caddie choice, so always verify a live quote before finalizing plans. Standard packages typically bundle the round with a cart or trolley, locker use, and sometimes a light lunch at the clubhouse restaurant — confirm inclusions when booking since this varies by plan. Visiting golfers in Japan most commonly book through Rakuten GORA or a similar Japanese golf-booking portal, or through a hotel concierge in Toyama City if staying nearby. ## Access / 접근·교통

Toyama Airport and Toyama Station (Hokuriku Shinkansen) are the practical arrival points, with the course a manageable drive from either — plan on roughly 30–45 minutes by car depending on traffic and exact route. A rental car is the more flexible option since tee times often start early and public transit to golf courses in this region is limited or nonexistent near the clubhouse. Taxis are workable for a one-way trip from central Toyama but can get expensive for a round trip, so most visiting golfers either rent a car for the day or arrange transport through their hotel. If you're combining this round with other Hokuriku stops, factor the drive time into your day rather than treating it as a quick add-on.

## Dress Code & Tips

Standard Japanese golf course etiquette applies: collared shirts, no denim, and soft spikes only — most clubhouses can rent shoes if you arrive without the right footwear. Pace of play tends to run on the traditional Japanese schedule with a clubhouse break between the front and back nine, so budget more time than a typical Western round. Three practical tips: book weekday mornings if you want a quieter pace and better rates; bring layers since mountain elevation means cooler mornings even in summer; and check weather closely in spring, since rain can affect turf conditions and cart availability more than at flatter courses.
