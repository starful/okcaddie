## Course Overview

Aomori Country Club sits in the hilly terrain outside Aomori city, and the "Mountain Style" tag is the honest way to describe it — expect elevation shifts, doglegs shaped by the land rather than bulldozed flat, and tree corridors that frame most holes. This kind of routing rewards course management over pure length: uneven lies, uphill approaches, and downhill run-outs are part of the test. Turf conditions in northern Tohoku typically lean toward cool-season grasses that green up through late spring and hold well into autumn, with a shorter playable window than courses further south. Wind and temperature swing more with elevation here than at coastal layouts, so mornings can start cool even in summer. Views toward the surrounding hills and, on clear days, out toward the coast are the payoff for the terrain — this is a course you play as much for the setting as the scorecard.

## Green Fees & Booking

Expect weekday green fees in the roughly ¥6,000–¥10,000 range and weekend/holiday fees somewhat higher, often ¥9,000–¥14,000, though this moves with season, cart vs. walking, and any lunch or onsen package bundled in. Peak autumn foliage weekends and midsummer weekends tend to sit at the top of that range; early spring or late-season weekdays are usually the best value. Packages at regional clubs like this one often include a cart, caddie or no-caddie option, and a set lunch at the clubhouse restaurant — always confirm what's bundled before you book. The simplest path for visitors is booking through Rakuten GORA or a similar Japanese golf reservation platform, or asking your hotel concierge in Aomori city to arrange it, since course websites and phone lines are often Japanese-only. ## Access

Aomori Airport (AOJ) is the closest air hub, with the course a reasonably short drive into the surrounding hills — plan on roughly 20 to 40 minutes depending on exact routing and traffic. From JR Shin-Aomori or Aomori Station, a taxi or rental car covers the same general drive time. A rental car is the more practical choice here: mountain-style courses like this are rarely a quick walk from a train stop, and having your own car lets you control tee time timing without depending on return taxi availability. If you're not driving, arrange a taxi in advance or ask your hotel to help book one, since taxis waiting at rural clubhouses aren't guaranteed.

## Dress Code & Tips

Standard Japanese golf club etiquette applies: collared shirts, tailored golf trousers or shorts, and soft spikes only — metal spikes are not permitted at essentially any Japanese course. Bring a light layer even in summer, since elevation and morning wind can run cooler than in Aomori city itself.

- Book your tee time and confirm cart/caddie options before you arrive — same-day walk-ins aren't reliable at regional clubs.
- Build in extra transit time going in; mountain roads and hill routing mean the drive can take longer than the map suggests.
- Pace of play on hillier layouts tends to run slower — plan for a longer round than a flat course and don't rush uphill approach shots.
