## Course Overview

Ishikawa Kaga Country sits inland in Kaga City, laid out through mature forest with rolling terrain rather than dramatic elevation swings. Fairways are framed tightly by trees on several holes, which rewards accuracy off the tee more than raw distance. Turf conditions in this part of Ishikawa typically favor cool-season grasses on greens with a firmer, well-drained feel through summer and early autumn, though exact maintenance schedules vary by season and recent weather. Wind is generally moderate given the tree cover, but expect pockets of exposure on higher ground. The "championship" tag reflects a layout built with tournament-caliber shot values — doglegs, strategic bunkering, and greens that punish careless approach angles — rather than sheer scorecard length.

## Access / 접근 · 교통

The nearest major access point is Kanazawa via Komatsu Airport, roughly a 30-40 minute drive from Komatsu to the Kaga area depending on traffic and exact course location. From Kanazawa city center, plan on about 45-60 minutes by car via the Hokuriku Expressway. Kaga Onsen Station on the JR line serves the area for those arriving by train, with a taxi transfer of roughly 15-20 minutes to most Kaga-area courses — confirm exact transfer time and cost with the course or your hotel, since it varies by specific pickup point. A rental car is the more flexible option for golfers combining multiple rounds or side trips to Kaga Onsen; taxis work fine for a single round but add up quickly for return trips, especially outside peak tourist hours when availability can be thin.

## Dress Code & Tips

Standard Japanese golf course etiquette applies: collared shirts, no denim, and tucked-in attire in the clubhouse and on course. Soft spikes are standard or required at most Ishikawa courses, so confirm before packing metal spikes. Bring a rain layer even in shoulder season, since forest courses hold moisture longer after a shower.

- Book tee times with buffer room — forest courses with tighter corridors can run slower pace of play, especially on weekends.
- Arrive at least 45-60 minutes early for check-in, locker assignment, and range warm-up, which is customary at most Japanese clubs.
- Pack extra balls; tree-lined holes here mean more chances of a wayward shot heading into rough or undergrowth.
