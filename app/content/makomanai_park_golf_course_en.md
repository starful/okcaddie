## Who This Course Is For

- First-time park golfers who want a low-pressure way to learn club handling and lag putting without the cost or pace pressure of a full golf course.
- Families and mixed-skill groups, since park golf uses a single club and a simplified scoring format that keeps everyone in the round.
- Travelers based in Sapporo who want a short, affordable outing that fits between sightseeing stops rather than eating up a full day.
- Skip it if you're chasing a full 18-hole golf experience with drivers and irons — this is a different sport played with one mallet-style club and a hard ball, not a scaled-down version of regular golf.
- Skip it if you need cart-only mobility assistance baked in; park golf courses are typically walked, so check on-site accessibility if that matters to you.

## Course Overview

Makomanai Park Golf Course sits inside a broader park setting in Minami Ward, giving the layout a relaxed, green, tree-lined character rather than the manicured fairways of a championship track. Park golf holes are short, generally bunched together, and run over flat to gently rolling ground, so elevation change and wind are rarely a major factor in how a hole plays. Turf is kept low and even for consistent roll, since the game is scored almost entirely on putter-style strokes and short approach shots. Expect a layout built for repeatable, quick rounds rather than dramatic shot-shaping — the challenge comes from distance control and reading subtle breaks, not from hazards or forced carries. Because it's a park-based facility, surrounding greenery and walking paths are part of the experience, and the course doubles as a pleasant stroll even between shots.

## Access

The course is located in Minami Ward on the southern side of Sapporo, making it a reasonable side trip from the city center rather than a remote destination. From New Chitose Airport, plan for roughly 45 minutes to just over an hour by car depending on traffic, since you'll be routing through or around the city. From central Sapporo (near Sapporo Station), expect something in the 30 to 40 minute range by car under normal conditions. A car is the most flexible way to reach the course given its park setting, but taxis are a workable backup if you're not renting a vehicle — just confirm the return trip, since park golf areas can have limited taxi presence outside peak hours. If you're relying on public transit, check current bus routes toward the Makomanai area and budget extra time for the walk from the nearest stop into the park.

## Dress Code & Tips

Dress is casual and practical — park golf doesn't enforce the collared-shirt expectations of standard golf courses, so comfortable athletic or outdoor clothing is fine. Soft-soled shoes or sneakers are appropriate; metal spikes are unnecessary and not typical for this format. A few practical tips: first, rent equipment on arrival if you're new to the sport rather than buying gear in advance, since rental is usually the cheaper way to try it once. Second, play ready golf — park golf groups move quickly, and keeping pace keeps the course enjoyable for everyone behind you. Third, bring sun protection and water in summer months, since much of the course is open parkland with limited shade, and check weather before winter and early spring visits when the course may be closed or snow-affected.
