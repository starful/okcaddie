# Spring Sakura Golf: Chasing Cherry Blossoms Across Chiba, Tokyo, and Mt. Fuji

For a few short weeks each spring, Japan's golf courses turn into something out of a woodblock print. Cherry trees planted decades ago along fairways and around clubhouses burst into bloom, and golfers who time their trip right get to play a round framed by pink canopies instead of just green turf. This guide focuses on three easy-to-combine regions around greater Tokyo — Chiba, the Tokyo commuter belt, and the Mt. Fuji foothills — where sakura season and golf season overlap perfectly.

## Why This Region for Sakura Golf

*   **Timing works in your favor.** Cherry blossoms typically peak in the Kanto area (Tokyo, Chiba, Kanagawa) in the last week of March through the first week of April, exactly when spring golf conditions — mild temperatures, dormant-to-green transitioning fairways, low humidity — are at their best.
*   **Everything is close together.** Chiba, central Tokyo, and the Fuji Five Lakes/Gotemba area are all within a 1-3 hour drive or train ride of each other, so a single trip can cover all three without long transfers.
*   **Mt. Fuji is often still snow-capped.** Late March/early April is one of the last windows to see cherry blossoms in the foreground with a snow-covered Fuji in the background — a combination that disappears by early summer.

## Chiba: Sakura-Lined Fairways Near Narita

Chiba's golf courses are numerous, well-maintained, and many line their approach roads and out-of-bounds areas with mature cherry trees.

*   **[Chiba Country Club Umesato](/course/chiba_country_club_umesato_en)** — A championship-pedigree forest course in Noda that has hosted the Japan Open. Its wooded holes make a strong backdrop for blooming cherry trees along the practice areas and clubhouse approach.
*   **[Narita Golf Club](/course/narita_golf_club_en)** — Extremely convenient if you're arriving via Narita Airport; play a sakura-season round on arrival or before departure without a major detour.

## Tokyo Day-Trip Courses

If you're based in central Tokyo and don't want to travel far, these courses are reachable without an overnight stay.

*   **[Tokyo Golf Club](/course/tokyo_golf_club_en)** — Historic and tree-lined, located in Sayama, Saitama, just outside the city. One of Japan's most storied clubs, with mature plantings that include cherry trees near the clubhouse grounds.

## Mt. Fuji View Courses: Blossoms in the Foreground, Fuji in the Back

This is the marquee combination of the trip — pink blossoms with Mt. Fuji rising behind the green.

*   **[Fuji Country Club](/course/fuji_country_club_en)** — Located in Gotemba at the base of Mt. Fuji, this historic course offers some of the most photographed Fuji views in Japanese golf.
*   **[Fujizakura Country Club](/course/fujizakura_country_club_en)** — Set in Fujikawaguchiko, Yamanashi, right in the Fuji Five Lakes area. "Fujizakura" literally references Fuji cherry blossoms, and the course sits at elevation with unobstructed Fuji views.
*   **[Taiheiyo Club Gotemba Course](/course/taiheiyo_club_gotemba_course_en)** — Another Gotemba-area option with a strong reputation and reliable Fuji sightlines on clear spring mornings.
*   **[Hiratsuka Fuji Golf Course](/course/hiratsuka_fuji_golf_course_en)** — In Kanagawa's Ashigarakami district, offering Fuji views from a different angle at a more accessible price point.

## Planning Your Sakura Golf Trip

*   **Book early.** Cherry blossom weekends are the single busiest booking period in the Kanto golf calendar — tee times at Fuji-view courses can sell out 4-6 weeks ahead.
*   **Watch the blossom forecast, not the calendar date.** Japan's meteorological agency and golf booking sites publish sakura forecasts every February; blossom timing shifts by up to two weeks year to year.
*   **Weekday rounds cost less and see fewer crowds** than weekend rounds during peak bloom — if your schedule allows, aim for a Tuesday-to-Thursday window.
*   **Layer your clothing.** Late March mornings near Mt. Fuji can still be near freezing even when Tokyo feels like spring; a windproof layer over your golf shirt is worth packing.
*   **Pack a camera or phone with a wide lens.** The best sakura + Fuji shots are usually from elevated tees, not the clubhouse.

## Sample 3-Day Itinerary

*   **Day 1:** Arrive Narita, play [Narita Golf Club](/course/narita_golf_club_en), overnight in Chiba or central Tokyo.
*   **Day 2:** Morning round at [Chiba Country Club Umesato](/course/chiba_country_club_umesato_en) or [Tokyo Golf Club](/course/tokyo_golf_club_en), afternoon transfer toward Gotemba/Fuji Five Lakes.
*   **Day 3:** Sunrise or late-morning round at [Fuji Country Club](/course/fuji_country_club_en) or [Fujizakura Country Club](/course/fujizakura_country_club_en) for the classic sakura-and-Fuji shot before heading back.

## Bottom Line

Sakura season golf near Tokyo rewards travelers who plan around the bloom forecast rather than fixed dates, book Fuji-view tee times well in advance, and are willing to layer up for cool mountain mornings. Chiba gives you convenience, the Tokyo belt gives you history, and the Fuji foothills give you the postcard shot — combine all three for a golf trip that only exists for a few weeks a year.
