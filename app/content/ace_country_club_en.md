ACE Country Club is a long-standing public layout in the Narita golf belt, the cluster of courses that grew up around the airport to serve both local weekend players and travelers with a few hours to spare before or after a flight.

## Who This Course Is For

- Golfers flying through Narita who want a round squeezed into a layover or the morning before departure
- Tokyo-based players looking for an easy public booking without joining a member-only club
- Groups that value a straightforward, no-frills golf day over a resort production
- Skip it if you're specifically chasing a famous-name design pedigree — this is a solid regional public track, not a signature-architect showcase
- Skip it if you're booking a tee time within two to three hours of an international departure; airport transfer and bag drop eat into that buffer fast

## Course Overview

The course runs through typical Chiba terrain — gentle elevation changes, stands of trees separating holes, and fairways that reward keeping the ball in play rather than bombing it. As with most courses in this region, expect a mix of seasonal turf conditions: firmer, faster fairways in the dry winter months, lusher growth and softer landing areas during the summer rainy season. Wind is rarely a dominant factor here compared with coastal Chiba courses, but morning rounds tend to play calmer than afternoon ones. The routing favors accuracy over raw length, with doglegs and tree lines doing more defensive work than deep bunkering or forced water carries. It's a course built for repeatable, comfortable rounds rather than one that tries to punish every miss.

## Green Fees & Booking

Expect roughly ¥8,000–¥14,000 on weekdays and ¥15,000–¥22,000 on weekends and holidays, though exact pricing shifts with season, time of day, and whether a cart or caddie is included. Weekday packages at Narita-area public courses often bundle a cart, lunch at the clubhouse, and sometimes a bath/onsen facility; weekend rates usually strip some of that down or price it separately. ## Access

The course sits only a short drive from Narita International Airport, making it one of the more realistic "round before or after your flight" options in the Tokyo area. From central Tokyo, figure on roughly 60–90 minutes by car depending on traffic and route, longer during rush hour on the Higashi-Kanto Expressway. From the airport itself, the drive is typically well under 30 minutes. A rental car or taxi is the practical choice — public transit options in this part of rural Chiba are limited and not built around golfer schedules, so don't plan on reaching the clubhouse by train and bus alone. If you're coming straight from a flight, allow extra time for baggage, rental car pickup, and the fact that Japanese rural roads are narrower and slower than GPS estimates suggest.

## Dress Code & Tips

Standard Japanese public course etiquette applies: collared shirts, no jeans or t-shirts, and soft spikes only — metal spikes are not accepted at the clubhouse or on the course. A few practical notes:

1. Arrive at least 45–60 minutes before your tee time for check-in, locker assignment, and the customary pre-round stretch or announcement.
2. Pace of play is taken seriously; a caddie or forecaddie, where offered, will help keep your group moving and is worth the extra cost if it's available on your slot.
3. Rental clubs and shoes are usually available but limited in sizes and brands, so travelers with less luggage room should reserve rentals in advance rather than assume walk-up availability.
